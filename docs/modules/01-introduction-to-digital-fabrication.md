# Module 01: Introduction to Digital Fabrication

## Purpose

Introduce digital fabrication as a workflow that connects design intent, computational models, machine constraints, material behavior, and documented iteration.

## Duration

60-90 minutes

## Learning outcomes

Participants will be able to:

- Define digital fabrication in relation to robotic fabrication.
- Identify the stages of a robotic fabrication workflow.
- Name common outputs, risks, and documentation artifacts.
- Explain why simulation, dry runs, and material tests are part of the process.

## Required materials

- Slide deck or visual examples
- Printed workflow diagram
- Example fabrication files or photos
- Whiteboard or shared digital notes

## Session plan

| Segment | Activity | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 min | Welcome and context | Shared goals |
| 15 min | Workflow overview | Annotated workflow diagram |
| 20 min | Example walkthrough | List of design and machine constraints |
| 20 min | Group discussion | Questions and assumptions |
| 15 min | Documentation prompt | Short reflection |

## Prompt

Ask participants to describe one object, pattern, or assembly they would like to fabricate robotically. Then ask them to list what the robot, tool, material, and documentation would need to know.

## Assessment

Participants should be able to describe the workflow in their own words and identify at least three points where decisions must be documented.

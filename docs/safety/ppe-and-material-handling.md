# PPE and Material Handling

## Purpose

This guide helps facilitators prepare personal protective equipment and material handling expectations for digital fabrication workshops.

## Common PPE categories

| Hazard | Example PPE or control |
| --- | --- |
| Eye impact | Safety glasses or face shield |
| Dust or particles | Dust extraction, respirator where approved |
| Heat | Heat-resistant gloves, tool cooldown time |
| Sharp edges | Cut-resistant gloves where appropriate |
| Noise | Hearing protection |
| Chemical exposure | Gloves, ventilation, safety data sheet review |

## Material handling checklist

- [ ] Material is approved for the tool and process.
- [ ] Safety data sheets are available where required.
- [ ] Storage conditions are known.
- [ ] Cutting, heating, melting, curing, or dust-producing risks are reviewed.
- [ ] Waste and cleanup procedure are clear.
- [ ] Participants know whether they may touch finished samples.
- [ ] Materials are labeled after testing.

## Documentation notes

For each workshop, record:

- Material name and supplier if relevant
- Batch, thickness, density, or moisture content if relevant
- Tool settings that affect material behavior
- Observed odor, dust, smoke, residue, cracking, warping, or deformation
- Disposal or cleanup notes

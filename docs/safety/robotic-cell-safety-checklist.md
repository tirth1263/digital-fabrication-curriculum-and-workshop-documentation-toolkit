# Robotic Cell Safety Checklist

Use this checklist before running a robot during a workshop.

## Access control

- [ ] Only authorized operators enter the controlled zone.
- [ ] Observation areas are clearly marked.
- [ ] Participants understand when the robot is live, paused, or powered down.
- [ ] The cell boundary is visible and respected.

## Emergency systems

- [ ] Emergency stop locations are identified.
- [ ] Emergency stops are tested according to local procedure.
- [ ] Operators know the restart procedure.
- [ ] A clear verbal stop command is agreed upon.

## Robot and tooling

- [ ] Tool is mounted securely.
- [ ] Payload is within robot and tooling limits.
- [ ] Tool center point and work frame are verified.
- [ ] Cables, hoses, and material feeds have clearance.
- [ ] Fixtures are secure.
- [ ] Sharp, hot, pressurized, or powered tools are controlled.

## Program review

- [ ] Simulation or offline review is complete.
- [ ] Collision risks are reviewed.
- [ ] Speeds are reduced for first run.
- [ ] Dry run is completed without process output.
- [ ] Approach and retract moves are verified.
- [ ] Operator has line of sight to the relevant risk area.

## Operation

- [ ] No one enters the controlled zone during motion.
- [ ] The operator remains at the station.
- [ ] Participants observe from the designated area.
- [ ] Any unexpected behavior triggers a stop and review.

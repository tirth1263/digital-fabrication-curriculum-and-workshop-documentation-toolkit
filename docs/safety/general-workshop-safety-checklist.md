# General Workshop Safety Checklist

Use this checklist before any workshop begins. Adapt it to local policy and equipment.

## Space readiness

- [ ] Walkways are clear.
- [ ] Emergency exits are accessible.
- [ ] Fire extinguishers and first aid supplies are visible and accessible.
- [ ] Electrical cables are secured or routed away from walkways.
- [ ] Ventilation requirements are met.
- [ ] Worktables are stable and uncluttered.
- [ ] Material storage is organized and labeled.

## Participant readiness

- [ ] Participants have received a safety briefing.
- [ ] Required PPE is available and worn correctly.
- [ ] Long hair, loose clothing, and jewelry are secured.
- [ ] Food and drink are outside fabrication areas.
- [ ] Participants know who is authorized to operate equipment.
- [ ] Participants know when and how to stop work.

## Instructional readiness

- [ ] Workshop goals and limits are stated.
- [ ] Machine-specific hazards are explained.
- [ ] Demonstration boundaries are marked.
- [ ] A technician or qualified operator is present when required.
- [ ] A documentation station is prepared.
- [ ] Cleanup responsibilities are assigned.

## Closeout

- [ ] Tools are returned.
- [ ] Materials are stored or disposed of correctly.
- [ ] Work surfaces are cleaned.
- [ ] Waste is labeled and removed.
- [ ] Incidents, near misses, or maintenance issues are documented.

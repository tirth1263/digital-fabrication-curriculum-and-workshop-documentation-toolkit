## Immediate response sequence

1. Stop the machine or process if it is safe to do so.
2. Move people away from the hazard.
3. Call emergency services or local response contacts as required.
4. Provide first aid only if trained and safe.
5. Preserve the scene for investigation if required.
6. Document the incident after people are safe.

## Incident documentation

Record:

- Date, time, and location
- People involved
- Equipment and material involved
- Description of what happened
- Immediate actions taken
- Photos if allowed by policy
- Follow-up actions
- Whether workshop procedures need revision

## Workshop stop conditions

Stop the workshop if:

- Emergency systems fail.
- The robot, tool, or fixture behaves unexpectedly.
- Required supervision is unavailable.
- Ventilation, PPE, or material controls are inadequate.
- Participants repeatedly cross safety boundaries.
- A near miss indicates that the activity plan is not safe enough.

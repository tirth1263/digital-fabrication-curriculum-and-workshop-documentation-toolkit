# Assessment Rubrics

Use these rubrics to evaluate workshop participation, project work, and documentation quality. Scores can be used informally for feedback or adapted for graded courses.

## Safety practice rubric

| Criteria | 1 - Needs support | 2 - Developing | 3 - Proficient | 4 - Advanced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PPE and shop conduct | Needs repeated reminders | Follows most requirements | Follows requirements consistently | Helps others follow requirements |
| Robot cell awareness | Unclear about restricted zones | Recognizes boundaries with prompts | Maintains safe distance and attention | Anticipates hazards and communicates clearly |
| Emergency readiness | Does not know emergency procedures | Can identify emergency stop locations | Can explain stop and escalation process | Supports calm, correct response planning |
| Material handling | Handles materials inconsistently | Handles materials safely with reminders | Handles and stores materials correctly | Connects handling to risk assessment |

## Process documentation rubric

| Criteria | 1 - Needs support | 2 - Developing | 3 - Proficient | 4 - Advanced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Variables recorded | Missing key variables | Records some variables | Records relevant variables and units | Explains why variables matter |
| Visual evidence | Few or unclear images | Captures partial process | Captures setup, process, and result | Uses images to support analysis |
| Failure notes | Omits failures | Notes failures generally | Describes likely causes | Proposes testable next steps |
| Summary quality | Hard to follow | Basic sequence is present | Clear, concise, and complete | Connects findings to future work |

## Fabrication outcome rubric

| Criteria | 1 - Needs support | 2 - Developing | 3 - Proficient | 4 - Advanced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Design-to-process fit | Workflow does not match intent | Some constraints considered | Workflow supports design intent | Workflow shows strong constraint reasoning |
| Toolpath logic | Motion strategy is unclear | Motion strategy is partly justified | Motion strategy is clear and repeatable | Strategy is optimized through evidence |
| Material response | Material behavior ignored | Material behavior observed | Material behavior informs changes | Material response drives iteration plan |
| Presentation | Outcome lacks context | Outcome has basic explanation | Outcome includes process and results | Outcome includes critique and recommendations |
